# White-label Cam Strategy

Updated: 2026-09-28

## Operating principle

StripUnion may operate branded white-label cam destinations when the underlying service is an official first-party operator program and the site has a differentiated acquisition purpose. White labels are not treated as independent editorial brands and must not replace StripUnion's comparison/review layer.

The editorial funnel remains:

```
Search / social / community
        ↓
StripUnion / StripUnion Blog
        ↓
comparison / review / pricing context
        ↓
direct official affiliate destination
        OR
owned branded white-label destination
```

## Streamate / CamBuilder

CamBuilder is Streamate's official affiliate and white-label platform.

### White Label

CamBuilder hosts and operates the white-label experience. The affiliate supplies a domain and can customize branding, layout, filters and model presentation. CamBuilder documents free hosting for unlimited white-label domains.

### Black Label

CamBuilder also provides deeper integration products:

- **Black Label Light** — model list/search/categories/live stream; registration, login and paid services stay with Streamate/white label.
- **Black Label Pro** — deeper site integration with sample code, excluding billing.
- **Black Label Blocks** — component-level integration.
- **Black Label API** — maximum integration flexibility, including own-wallet billing, but requires substantially more development.

### Verified payout options

CamBuilder currently documents:

- 35% Lifetime Revenue Share
- $100 Per Spender
- $40 Per Join
- $75 Per Spender + 30% Lifetime Revenue Share
- $30 Per Join + 30% Lifetime Revenue Share

CamBuilder itself states that the standalone Lifetime Revenue Share pays the most over time, while fixed Per Spender / Per Join programs are more front-loaded.

### Important domain-ownership rule

For the standalone **35% Lifetime Revenue Share**, CamBuilder's current help documentation says the standard domain/DNS configuration is sufficient.

For payout programs other than standalone Lifetime Revenue Share, CamBuilder requires the white-label domain to be transferred to CamBuilder before payouts begin.

For StripUnion, this means:

- prefer an expendable/portfolio domain rather than the core `stripunion.com` or `blog.stripunion.com`;
- if testing CamBuilder, default analysis should start with **35% Lifetime Revenue Share** because it avoids transferring ownership and aligns with long-term affiliate economics;
- do not transfer a strategically important domain merely to access a one-time/hybrid payout model.

## Experiment policy

Do not declare CamBuilder superior solely because 35% > 20%.

Compare each white-label on:

- visitors sent;
- signup / qualified-join rate;
- first spender rate;
- average spend and repeat spend;
- revenue per 1,000 sessions;
- geo/device performance;
- landing-page bounce and return rate;
- SEO / brand-domain traffic;
- payout restrictions and domain-control cost.

A lower nominal revshare can outperform a higher one if conversion, payment methods, model-market fit or repeat spending are materially stronger.
