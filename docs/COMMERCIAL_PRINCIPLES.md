# StripUnion Commercial Principles

## Primary objective

StripUnion exists to build profitable, compounding affiliate revenue from high-intent adult-platform traffic.

Technology, editorial structure, SEO and UX are means to that objective, not ends in themselves.

## Traffic rule

Do not send valuable users to third-party destinations that do not produce measurable commercial value.

Allowed user-facing outbound destinations:

1. approved affiliate destinations
2. StripUnion-owned domains, including `blog.stripunion.com`

Research sources used to validate claims should be kept in internal research notes or editorial working documents rather than exposed as clickable exits when there is no commercial reason to send traffic away.

## Editorial strategy

Editorial content still needs to be useful and credible because search traffic and trust are prerequisites for conversion.

Use non-affiliate brands to capture search intent through:

- reviews
- comparisons
- alternatives
- best-of guides
- pricing/how-it-works pages

But keep users inside StripUnion until there is a clear monetizable next step.

## CTA strategy

Every high-intent page should have:

- a commercial CTA above the fold when context supports it
- a contextual mid-page CTA
- a closing CTA
- compact, compliant paid-link disclosure without repetitive legalistic copy
- source attribution for click analytics

Do not use dark patterns or disguise one destination as another.

## Current commercial destination

Until additional affiliate programs are approved, the only third-party commercial destination promoted by StripUnion is the approved Stripchat / StripCash tracking URL configured centrally in the codebase.

## Affiliate expansion

Apply for additional programs when traffic proves demand for a specific platform or category. After approval, introduce that platform-specific affiliate destination and track its EPC/conversion performance against existing offers.


## Disclosure UX

Do not place large affiliate-disclosure boxes, repeated commission explanations or footer disclosure links across the commercial funnel.

For outbound affiliate CTAs, use the compact `Paid link` label immediately adjacent to the CTA and keep `rel="sponsored"` on the outbound link.

Keep the full legal notice available as a noindex fallback page, but do not use it as a primary navigation or SEO destination.
